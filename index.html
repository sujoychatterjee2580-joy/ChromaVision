Genetics & Etiology

Normal Color Trichromacy

Normal vision relies on three functional photopigments located within retinal cone photoreceptors: L-cones (peak sensitivity ~560nm, red), M-cones (~530nm, green), and S-cones (~420nm, blue). This triad enables the human brain to distinguish over 1 million color nuances.

Global Female Prevalence: ~99.5% Normal
